   五、押题翻译（原文 + 高分译文）
   预测 1：剪纸
   原文剪纸是中国最古老的民间艺术之一，有着超过 2000 年的历史。它主要用于节日装饰和婚礼庆典，象征着幸福与吉祥。随着时代发展，剪纸不仅保留了传统特色，还融入了现代元素，深受国内外人们的喜爱。
   译文Paper-cutting is one of the oldest folk arts in China, with a history of more than 2,000 years. It is mainly used for festival decorations and wedding ceremonies, symbolizing happiness and good fortune. With the development of the times, paper-cutting not only keeps its traditional features but also integrates modern elements, making it popular among people at home and abroad.
   预测 2：中国城市化
   原文近年来，中国城市化进程不断加快，越来越多的人涌入城市。政府致力于改善公共设施，提高居民生活质量。可持续发展已成为社会共识，旨在实现经济与环境的平衡。
   译文In recent years, the process of urbanization in China has been accelerating, and more and more people are flooding into cities. The government is committed to improving public facilities and enhancing the quality of life for residents. Sustainable development has become a social consensus, aiming to achieve a balance between the economy and the environment.
   预测 3：在线教育
   原文互联网极大地改变了人们的学习方式。在线教育凭借其便捷性和灵活性，受到广大学生的欢迎。它不仅提供了丰富的学习资源，还促进了教育公平，让更多人有机会接受优质教育。
   译文The Internet has greatly changed the way people learn. With its convenience and flexibility, online education is popular among a large number of students. It not only provides abundant learning resources but also promotes educational equality, enabling more people to have access to quality education.
   预测 4：中国书法
   原文中国书法是中国特有的传统艺术，已有数千年历史。它不仅是一种书写方式，更是一种表达情感和思想的艺术形式。如今，书法依然受到人们的喜爱，许多学校开设书法课程，以传承这一珍贵文化。
   译文Chinese calligraphy is a unique traditional art in China, with a history of thousands of years. It is not only a way of writing but also an art form expressing emotions and thoughts. Nowadays, calligraphy is still popular among people, and many schools offer calligraphy courses to inherit this precious culture.
   预测 5：中国高铁
   原文近年来，中国高铁发展迅速，成为世界领先水平。高铁以其高速、安全和舒适的特点，极大地改变了人们的出行方式。它不仅促进了区域经济发展，也加强了城市之间的联系。
   译文In recent years, China’s high-speed railway has developed rapidly and become a world leader. With its high speed, safety and comfort, high-speed rail has greatly changed the way people travel. It not only promotes regional economic development but also strengthens connections between cities.
